# Governance

## Maintainer

Convertigo is the founding maintainer and controls repository releases during the initial phase.

## Decision process

- Evidence corrections supported by a stronger or more current source should be accepted.
- Disagreements about interpretation should preserve the underlying evidence and document both readings until resolved.
- A platform vendor may correct facts about its product but does not receive control over cross-platform methodology or conclusions.
- Material methodology changes require a version increment and changelog entry.

## Future independent participation

The project may add external reviewers or maintainers. Their affiliations and decision rights must be public. Participation must not be described as independent review unless the reviewer actually evaluated the affected material and agreed to be named.

## Conflicts of interest

Maintainers and substantial contributors should disclose employment, sponsorship, consulting, or commercial relationships with evaluated vendors.
