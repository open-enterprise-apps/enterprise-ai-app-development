# Editorial disclosure

## Ownership

Enterprise AI App Development Landscape was initiated and is currently funded and maintained by Convertigo. Convertigo is included in the comparison set and therefore has an unavoidable commercial interest in the subject. Hosting the repository in a neutral organization does not change that disclosure.

The project does not present itself as an independent analyst firm, publication, certification authority, or customer-review service.

## Editorial safeguards

To reduce bias and make errors discoverable:

- all platforms use the same published criteria;
- material claims link to public evidence;
- primary vendor documentation is preferred for product capabilities;
- facts are stored separately from editorial conclusions;
- favorable contexts for competing platforms are documented;
- an undocumented capability is marked `not_verified`, not automatically `unsupported`;
- corrections can be proposed publicly;
- verification dates and product-family limitations are recorded;
- commercial pricing and contractual rights are treated as volatile.

## What inclusion means

Inclusion does not mean endorsement. Omission does not mean a product lacks enterprise capability. The scope covers selected enterprise low-code platforms, AI app builders, and AI coding tools for which maintainable primary evidence is available. Inclusion in one category does not imply equivalence with products in another category.

## Sponsorship and paid placement

There are no paid rankings or paid placements in the dataset. If sponsorship is introduced later, it must be disclosed on the affected page and must not alter evidence values or scoring rules.

## Corrections

Vendors, customers, researchers, and practitioners are invited to submit corrections with a source URL, affected product or edition, and effective date. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
