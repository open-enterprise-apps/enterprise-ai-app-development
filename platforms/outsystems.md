# OutSystems

Last evidence review: 2026-07-30

## Documented position

OutSystems is an enterprise platform for full-stack applications, modernization, and integrated application lifecycle management. Its current offerings include AI-assisted development and agent-oriented capabilities.

## Strong-fit contexts

- Large portfolios of mission-critical applications
- Organizations seeking integrated lifecycle governance and DevSecOps
- Modernization programs requiring a mature enterprise platform and operating model
